# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

RyanOS is committed to keeping the RyanPurge community professional, respectful, and useful. Participants must communicate in good faith and must not harass, threaten, impersonate, discriminate against, or deliberately expose private information about another person.

## Expected behavior

Use clear and constructive language. Keep discussions focused on the project. Respect different levels of technical experience. When reporting a problem, describe the behavior accurately and avoid personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, spam, malicious files, credential requests, impersonation of RyanOS, and attempts to distribute unofficial or rebranded RyanPurge builds while presenting them as official.

## Enforcement

Reports may be sent privately to RyanOS through the official Discord community linked from the project website. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, close discussions, or block accounts when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and to interactions made in the project’s name.
